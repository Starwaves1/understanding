### box — highlight something ON THE USER'S SCREEN
Text (preferred): `{"op":"box","quote":"The ratio of the mass-flow of air bypassing","text":"the definition"}`
Region: `{"op":"box","x":120,"y":300,"w":420,"h":60,"text":"this chart"}`
For TEXT on screen (a sentence, a line of code, a menu item), give `quote`: the exact words as they appear, copied character for character, 3–12 words; it is located precisely and highlighted like a marker, even across lines. Use x/y/w/h only for non-text regions (images, charts). Optional short `text` label.
